## 4. Competitor Tier Breakdown & SWOT Analysis

### Tier 1: Large Multi-Specialty Hospital Chains (Apollo, Yashoda, KIMS, CARE)
- **Strengths:** High brand trust, physical infrastructure, 24/7 emergency & ICU care, insurance/TPA networks.
- **Weaknesses:** Complex navigation on digital portals, fragmented booking flows across branches, opaque procedure pricing, long outpatient (OPD) wait times.

### Tier 2: Digital HealthTech Aggregators (Practo, MediBuddy, Lybrate)
- **Strengths:** Seamless UI/UX, instant doctor discovery, verified patient reviews, integrated teleconsultation and lab test delivery.
- **Weaknesses:** Lack of real-time hospital bed/emergency availability, limited integration with in-hospital billing/admission workflows.

## 5. Identified Market Gaps & Strategic Opportunities
1. **Real-Time Bed & Emergency Triage Visibility:** Neither major hospital websites nor aggregators display live ICU/bed availability or real-time ER wait times.
2. **Transparent Cost Estimation:** Patients struggle to compare surgery or treatment package costs upfront across hospitals.
3. **Unified Post-Care & OPD Queue Tracking:** Opportunity to reduce physical waiting room congestion with live token tracking and digital discharge summaries.
4. **Hyper-Local Discovery:** Many mid-sized local hospitals have strong ratings in `hospitals.csv` but weak digital booking presence compared to corporate chains.
